Making & Sharing

A shared, editable annual Gantt. Place workshops, courses, residencies and public events wherever they make sense. Lanes are yours to define.

Connecting to shared board…
On GitHub Pages, people visiting the same URL can edit the same timetable.
Arrange your year: Drag any block to another month or lane. On touch screens, select a block and tap its destination. Double-click a block to edit its title, month, duration or details. Everything in the Gantt is shared when PlayHTML connects.
